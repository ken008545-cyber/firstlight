# Firstlight: Writing and Leaving Books
Draft by Techknight The Scribe for Mr Knight.

## Crafting
- **Blank Book:** 2 Hide (cover), 3 Fiber, 1 Clay (ink). Stone Age.
- **Writing Desk:** a Keflings-style building (Wood, Stone). You write at it.
- **Lectern:** a small placeable (Wood, Stone) that holds one book in the world.

## Writing
1. Use a Blank Book at a Writing Desk, and the parchment reader opens in edit mode.
2. Give it a title, then pages (suggested caps: 50 pages, about 500 characters each, to keep saves small).
3. **Sign** it with the author's name. A signed book is sealed and can't be edited, so what's left in the world stays as written. Unsigned drafts stay in the inventory.

## Leaving and finding
- Place a signed book on a Lectern or a shelf anywhere. It glows softly in torchlight.
- Anyone who walks up can tap or press E to read it. Reading never removes it.
- Books are saved in the world save, and in co-op the host's world holds them, so friends find what you left.
- The Bible is special. It can't be edited or destroyed, it loads one chapter at a time, and only the Lord's own words show in red, per Brother's standing rule.

## Lore tie-in
Books are how the tribe remembers in a world of forgetting (the Fog). Villagers near a placed book sometimes speak a line from it as an idle line.

## Safety for a public link
Before player books travel between players (co-op or shared), add a simple word filter and a "hide this book" option for the host, since someone's own words go into a friend's world.
