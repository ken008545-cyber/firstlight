## First 5 quests
1. **Keep the Fire** — Gather 5 Wood and bring it to the campfire.
   Goal: teaches gathering and hauling. Reward: 1 villager joins the tribe.
   Line: "The fire is dying. Feed it, or the night takes us."
2. **Carry the Light** — Craft 2 Torches (1 Wood, 1 Fiber each) and place one at the fog's edge.
   Goal: teaches crafting and fog clearing. Reward: fog pulls back around the cave.
   Line: "Light a torch, and carry the light outward."
3. **Stone Teeth** — Gather 2 Flint and craft a Flint Axe.
   Goal: teaches tools. Reward: villagers chop faster.
   Line: "The trees are stubborn. Give our hands sharper teeth."
4. **Store the Harvest** — Gather 6 Berries and 3 Clay, then craft a Clay Pot at the campfire.
   Goal: teaches food and storage. Reward: food bar fills and berries stop spoiling.
   Line: "Berries rot by morning. Make them a home."
5. **The First Night** — Craft a Flint Spear and 2 Hide Wraps, then survive until dawn.
   Goal: teaches defense and night survival. Reward: the age-progress bar opens and a new villager joins at sunrise.
   Line: "The night has teeth. Tonight, so do we."
